Why it exists

Searching takes time. Early postings go fast.

  • 63%

    of students in Germany work alongside their studies.

    DZHW, 22nd Sozialerhebung, nearly 188,000 students, data from summer 2021. [1]
  • 41%

    of applications arrive within 48 hours of a job being posted. 56% arrive within 96 hours.

    St. Louis Fed analysis of about 80 million applications to nearly 10 million postings. [2]
  • 8.4 hrs

    a week is what unemployed job seekers spend searching.

    New York Fed Survey of Consumer Expectations, pooled 2013 to 2015 waves. Employed searchers spend 1.2 hours. [3]

The second and third figures come from US studies, and the Fed's application data is from 2017. I haven't found German equivalents. They show that speed and search time matter. They don't prove that applying early gets you more interviews.

How scoring works

  • Each posting gets a score from 0 to 100 against your form answers and your CV.
  • Only 70 and above is sent, up to five a day.
  • 70 is JobPing's own cutoff, not an industry standard. It keeps the digest to postings worth your time.
  • No match clears the bar? You get a short note instead of filler.

Boards scanned: Indeed, LinkedIn, StepStone, Xing, Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Jobvector and BerlinStartupJobs. Mostly-German boards are checked against the German level you report.

Questions

Before you pay

Do I need to speak German?

No. Indeed, LinkedIn and many StepStone listings are in English. Boards that are mostly German are checked against the German level on your form.

What if there are no good matches?

You get a short note saying so, with a quote for the day. If nothing new was posted anywhere, the note says it was a slow posting day.

Where does my CV go?

You send it straight to me. It is not uploaded to a form.

How do I cancel?

Tell me before your renewal date. If you cancel after renewing, that month still runs to the next renewal date, and part months aren't refunded.
